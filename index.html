Is it really free?
Yes. Getting featured costs nothing right now. We're onboarding a first group of UAE shops, and early partners stay front and center as PartHunter grows.
What do I get?
Your stock on the homepage and at the top of relevant searches, in front of UAE owners actively looking for exactly what you sell. Every click goes to your store.
What's a product feed?
A file your store generates automatically with names, prices, images and links. Shopify and WooCommerce produce one with a single setting. Send the link and we read it live.
Do I change anything on my side?
No. You keep selling exactly as you do now. PartHunter points buyers at your existing store. We don't take orders, payments or a cut.
Can you edit my prices?
No, and not by policy alone: we read your store live rather than keeping a copy, so there's nothing on our side to edit. What you set is what shows.
How do I join?
Email partner@parthunter.ae, or tap the button and we'll open a ready-to-send message with the details we need.
